Privacy

What we do with your information

Short version: we use it to answer you, and that's all. We don't sell it and we don't share it with anybody who isn't helping us deliver your message.

What we collect

Only what you type into a form on this site:

  • Booking requests — your name, email, phone number, event date, guest count, service type and any details you write.
  • Email list — your email address, nothing else.

There are no accounts, no logins and no shopping cart on this site, so there is nothing else to collect.

What we do with it

Booking requests are emailed to Chef Joe so he can send you a quote and answer your questions. Email-list addresses are used to send occasional notes about where the truck will be parked. That is the whole list of uses.

We do not sell your information, rent it, or hand it to advertisers.

Who else sees it

Form submissions pass through formsubmit.co, a service that forwards them to our inbox, and then sit in our email provider's servers like any other message. Those two companies process the message so it can reach us — they are not given it for their own use.

If you'd rather not use the form at all, call or text (225) 371-5951, or email bayoueatz@outlook.com directly.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. Nothing follows you around after you leave.

Two things do load from outside: the page's typefaces come from Google Fonts, and forms post to the service named above. Neither is used to track you across other sites.

How long we keep it

Booking requests stay in our email so we have a record of what was agreed. Ask us to delete yours and we will.

Getting off the list, or getting your information removed

Email bayoueatz@outlook.com and say the word. No forms, no hoops — we'll take you off and confirm it.

Children

This site isn't aimed at children and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes up on this page.
