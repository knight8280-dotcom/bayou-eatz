      Our story

      Big flavor, small window.

      Bayou Eatz brings New Orleans energy to every lot we park in. Mardi Gras colors on the truck, Patton's hot sausage on the griddle, and a Bayou Sauce recipe we're not sharing with anybody.

      Everything comes out of the fryer to order — no heat lamps, no holding trays. That's why the line moves the way it does, and why the loaded fries are worth the wait.

      Roll up hungry. Leave with your hands full.

      New plates go up most days on @bayoueatzz and Facebook.

        Before you ask

        Questions we get

        Anything not covered here — call or text Chef Joe at (225) 371-5951.

        Where is the truck today?

        Check the booking calendar on this page — public stops are marked in gold. We also post day-of updates and sellouts on Facebook, since spots can change with the weather.

        Do you take cards?

        Yes. Cash and all major cards are accepted at the window.

        Can I book the truck for a private event?

        That's a big part of what we do — birthdays, weddings, company lunches, church functions, graduations and block parties. Send a request through the form on this page, or call or text Chef Joe directly.

        How far in advance should I book?

        Two weeks is comfortable, and weekends fill up first. If your date is sooner than that, call — we can sometimes make it work.

        How far will you travel?

        We serve the greater Baton Rouge area as standard, and we'll travel further for the right event. Travel fees depend on distance, so call or email for a quote.

        What do you need at the venue?

        A flat, level spot roughly 30 feet long by 12 feet wide with clear overhead space, and room for guests to line up safely away from traffic. We run self-contained, so power isn't usually required — tell us about your site when you book and we'll confirm.

        Is there a minimum or a deposit?

        Both depend on the size of the event, the date and how far we're travelling. Send your details through the form and you'll get a written quote with everything spelled out.

        Can you handle allergies or dietary needs?

        Tell us when you order and we'll do what we can. Be aware that everything is fried in shared oil, so we can't promise a kitchen free of any particular allergen.

        Do you sell out?

        It happens, especially late in a service. Everything is cooked to order in small batches — when a item's gone for the day, it's gone. Following us on Facebook is the best way to catch the warning.
