    Our story

    Big flavor, small window.

    Bayou Eatz brings New Orleans energy to every lot we park in. Mardi Gras colors on the truck, Patton's hot sausage on the griddle, and a Bayou Sauce recipe we're not sharing with anybody.

    Everything comes out of the fryer to order — no heat lamps, no holding trays. That's why the line moves the way it does, and why the loaded fries are worth the wait.

    Roll up hungry. Leave with your hands full.

    New plates go up most days on @bayoueatzz and Facebook.

    • Made to order — nothing sits, everything comes out hot.
    • Signature Bayou Sauce — on the burger, on the fries, on request.
    • Real Patton's sausage — the New Orleans standard, no substitutes.
